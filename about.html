About XuanJi Technology

Nearly 20 years of professional international trade experience, we understand every aspect of global business. Established in 2005, XuanJi Technology is a professional company specializing in Chinese export trade, dedicated to helping worldwide clients find high-quality Chinese suppliers and build long-term stable partnerships.

Our core business covers three major areas: Biomedical Equipment, Auto Parts, and Industrial Testing Instruments. These are fields where our team has accumulated rich experience through years of trade practice. We deeply understand the technical details, quality standards, and market requirements of each product.

Sincerity, Integrity, and Win-Win are the most valuable experiences that have earned us customer trust over the past 20 years, and they are the business philosophy we always uphold. We believe that only by treating every customer sincerely can we build long-term cooperation; only by operating with integrity can we earn market respect; only by achieving win-win results can cooperation sustain and grow.

Our professional trade team has extensive international business experience and can provide comprehensive services to clients: from helping select suitable factories, to monitoring production progress, to strict quality control. We are involved throughout the entire process to ensure every step meets our clients' requirements.

Whether you are seeking OEM/ODM customization services or need bulk procurement of standard products, we will do our utmost to assist you, making your sourcing journey easy and worry-free.
